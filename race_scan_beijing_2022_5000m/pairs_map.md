# Beijing 2022 Men's 5000m — Pair Map for 5f Labeling

Source: `data/beijing_2022_5000m.mp4` (720p, 30 fps, 2 h 10 min).
Built 9/29 from the scan thumbnails (`sheets/sheet_00-06.jpg`). Sheets 07-13
(the later pairs, after the ice-resurfacing break at ~47:30-62:50) are not
yet mapped.

**Lap numbers** come from the on-screen race clock (bottom right):
`lap = floor((clock_seconds - 18) / 30) + 1` (first 200 m ≈ 18 s, then ~30 s
laps). This reproduced every on-screen "N LAPS TO GO" graphic checked
(lap = 13 - N). 5f splits each skater's laps into early / middle / late
thirds automatically.

**Identity**: by suit, confirmed against the on-screen name graphics. Skip any
stretch where both skaters of the pair are in frame.

**Phase calls (corner / straightaway) are the researcher's** — the side
rail-cam can make corners look like straights (9/28).
