# Double-push feedback: Mark Horsten (slow motion)

22 four-second windows judged. Compared with the elite reference (Joey Mantia and Viktor Hald Thorup). An issue is listed when it shows up in at least half of the windows.

## What to work on

### Skate lands too far out to the side
*Seen in 55% of windows.*

Set your skate down directly under your hips, not out to the side. Stepping wide forces a sharp zig-zag that kills your forward momentum instead of creating a smooth S-curve.

- **Cue:** Land on the centerline.
- **Drill:** Line-Straddle Drills: skate down a straight track line, making sure your set-down foot lands right along or across the center line.

## Not judged on this clip

- Jerky foot path (pressure spike, let go, then regained) (slow motion changes timing-based measures)
- Hips bouncing up and down (slow motion changes timing-based measures)
- No fall and catch (body stays upright over the skate) (slow motion changes timing-based measures)
